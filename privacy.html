Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

Wasipy is an AI assistant that works inside the Google account you connect. It can read your calendar, emails, files, spreadsheets and documents, and — only after you confirm — create events, send emails, add rows and create documents for you. This policy explains what data Wasipy collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. What we collect

  • Google account information — your name, email address and profile picture, provided by Google when you sign in.
  • Google access credentials — the secure tokens Google issues when you connect a service (Calendar, Gmail, Drive, Sheets or Docs). Each service is connected separately, and you choose which ones to connect.
  • Google content you ask about — calendar events, emails, files, spreadsheet rows and documents that Wasipy reads only when you ask it to, in order to answer you or carry out your instruction.
  • Things you create in Wasipy — your chat messages with the assistant and the reminders you set.

Wasipy does not collect your Google password, does not track you across other websites, and does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

2. How your data is used

Your data is used only to provide the features you ask for: the assistant reads your connected Google services to answer your questions, and performs actions (such as booking a meeting or sending an email) only after you confirm them. To understand your requests, your messages and the content needed to answer them are processed by an AI provider (OpenAI). Your data is never used to train advertising models, and it is never sold.

Use of Google user data. Wasipy's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide or improve the user-facing features you request. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with other parties except the service providers needed to run Wasipy (hosting, database and AI processing). No person reads your Google data unless you give consent, or it is necessary for security, to comply with the law, or to protect against harm.

3. How your data is protected

  • Google access credentials are stored encrypted, and are never shown in the browser.
  • Every record is tied to your own user account. No user can ever see another user's data.
  • Sign-in sessions are protected with signed cookies, and all connections use encrypted HTTPS.
  • Write actions (creating events, sending emails, editing sheets or documents) always ask you to confirm first.

4. Sharing

Wasipy does not sell or share your personal data with third parties for their own purposes. Data is processed only by the service providers required to operate Wasipy (cloud hosting, database storage and AI processing), who are bound to handle it only as instructed and to keep it confidential.

5. Retention and deletion

  • When you disconnect a Google service, its stored credentials are deleted and Wasipy can no longer access that service.
  • Using “New chat” deletes your chat history.
  • Reminders are deleted when you cancel them.
  • You can ask for full deletion of your Wasipy account and all stored data at any time by emailing us at the address below. You can also remove Wasipy's access at any time from your Google Account under Security → Third-party access.

6. Cookies

Wasipy uses a single signed session cookie to keep you signed in. It does not use tracking, advertising or third-party cookies.

7. Children's privacy

Wasipy is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will be announced on the Wasipy website.

9. Contact
